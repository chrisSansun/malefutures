Men doing well is good for everyone.

Male Futures Foundation works for the health and wellbeing of men and boys in Sri Lanka, because when men are doing well, their families, workplaces and communities do better too.

Who we are

A foundation for the long run.

Male Futures Foundation is being set up as a not-for-profit company in Sri Lanka. It exists to keep the work going all year, not just on one day: events, workshops, campaigns and practical support that help men look after their health and each other.

Our first initiative is IMD Sri Lanka, the home of International Men’s Day here. It is led by Chris Murray, the Sri Lanka Ambassador appointed by the day’s founder, Dr Jerome Teelucksingh.

Why it matters

Men are struggling quietly, and it costs everyone.

Most of the men behind these numbers never told anyone they were in trouble.

5:1

Sri Lankan men die by suicide at about five times the rate of women. In 2022 that was 27 per 100,000 men and 5 per 100,000 women.

6 yrs

Men in Sri Lanka live around six years less than women: 74.4 years against 80.7.

50+

The highest male suicide rates are in men aged 50 and over, and male suicide by hanging has been rising since 2017.

Sources: Suicide trends in Sri Lanka, 1997 to 2022, Sri Lanka Journal of Psychiatry, UN World Population Prospects via GeoRank.

What we do

Practical, year-round, and open to everyone.

We start with the small things that get men talking and looking after themselves.

  • Events and talks

    An annual evening on International Men’s Day, and smaller events through the year that put leaders, experts and men from every background in one room.

  • Workshops

    Practical sessions for workplaces, schools and communities on health, fatherhood and talking about what is hard.

  • Campaigns

    Simple public campaigns that give people something to do, like asking a man how he really is, and asking twice.

  • Guides and role models

    Plain, practical guidance, and a spotlight on the good men who show up for the people around them.

Three days a year

Three days, one purpose.

  1. 31st January

    International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Men and Boys

    Recognising the violence men and boys experience, and where they can find support.

  2. 16th May

    World Day of the Boy Child

    Putting the education, safety and development of boys on the agenda.

  3. 19th November

    International Men’s Day

    The main day each year, with talks, workshops and community activity.

Not men versus women

The Future Is Everyone.

That is the 2026 theme of International Men’s Day, and it is how we work. Men doing better is good for their partners, daughters, mothers and colleagues. Supporting men and boys takes nothing away from anyone.

Most men are not short of people who care about them. They are short of someone who asks twice.
